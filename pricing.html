Frequently Asked Questions

How does billing work?

Plans are billed monthly or yearly. Yearly plans include a discount. You can switch between billing cycles or upgrade/downgrade anytime.

Can I switch plans?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately and billing adjusts accordingly.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Cancel at any time and your plan stays active until the end of your billing period. No hidden fees.

What's the Designer free tier?

Designers can start free with the Explore tier. You'll need to upgrade after $100 in earnings, 10 sales, or 3 active months.
