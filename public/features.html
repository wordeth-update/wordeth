03

Token Economy

A built-in currency system that powers creator monetization, content gating, and platform engagement.

Earn

Subscribers receive monthly token grants based on their plan tier. Extra token packs are available for purchase.

Spend

Use tokens to join gated rooms, play paid replays, or boost your content for more visibility.

Cash Out

Creators convert token earnings to real money through monthly payouts. Rates reflect platform activity and grow with the community.

Gated Rooms

Creators set a token price for live Verses rooms. Tokens are deducted from the listener and credited to the creator upon joining. Non-subscribers see a prompt to view subscription plans.

Paid Replays

Replay token prices are set independently (typically lower than live). Each play generates a token transfer logged in both the TokenLedger and EventsLedger for full auditability.

Token Burn (Boost)

When creators boost replays, those tokens are burned (removed from circulation), creating deflationary pressure and funding platform growth. They don't transfer to anyone.

Tokens Never Expire

Unlike many platforms that expire virtual currency, Wordeth tokens are permanent. Your balance carries forward month after month, so you can save up or spend freely.

Creator Payouts

Tokens Earned Real Money

Creators receive monthly payouts based on their token earnings. Payout rates are dynamic and reflect platform growth, listening activity, and community engagement.

Room Ratings

Attendee-driven quality signals that help listeners discover the best content and reward great creators.

Post-Room Rating

After spending at least 60 seconds in a room, users see an interactive rating prompt when they leave.

4 out of 5
Great Host Good Music Lively Chat Informative Entertaining Professional

Verified Attendance

Only real attendees can rate. The server validates each rating against the room's participant history, preventing fake reviews. A compound unique index ensures one rating per user per room.

Minimum Time60 seconds in room
ValidationServer-side participant history check
DuplicatesPrevented by compound unique index

Dual Aggregation

Each rating updates two averages: the individual replay's rating score AND the creator's overall profile rating. Both are visible on room cards and replay cards throughout the platform.

12

Privacy & Trust

Robust security measures and transparent policies that protect users and their data.

Comprehensive privacy policy covering data collection, usage, and user rights

JWT Authentication

Secure token-based authentication with 7-day expiry. Passwords hashed with bcrypt. Separate auth flows for users, advertisers, and partners.

Security Headers

Helmet.js enforces Content Security Policy. Express-rate-limit prevents abuse. CORS configured for trusted origins. Trust proxy enabled for accurate client identification.

Legal Compliance

Full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. Cookie consent banner with granular controls. Privacy admin tools for data management and compliance requests.

Cookie Controls

Users can choose between "Accept All" and "Essential Only" cookie preferences. The persistent cookie banner ensures informed consent before any non-essential tracking begins.

Events Ledger

An immutable events ledger tracks all financial transactions: token purchases, room earnings, replay plays, boost purchases, and payout calculations. Full audit trail for transparency.
