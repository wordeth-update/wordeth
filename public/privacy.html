Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 7, 2026 — Version 1.0

Wordeth ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and password when you register
  • Profile Information: Bio, avatar image, and social connections
  • User Content: Annotations, custom merchandise designs, and other content you create
  • Communications: Messages you send to us for support or feedback

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, search queries, lyrics viewed, and interaction patterns
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, and general device characteristics
  • IP Address: Used for security, rate limiting, and approximate location
  • Cookies & Local Storage: Used for session management, preferences, and analytics (see Section 5)

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Social Login: If you sign in with X (Twitter), Instagram, or Facebook, we receive your name, email, and public profile as authorized by you

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Provide and improve the Wordeth platform and its features
  • Authenticate your identity and maintain account security
  • Personalize your experience (e.g., search history, recommendations)
  • Analyze platform usage to improve features and performance
  • Display contextual advertisements based on content (not personal data)
  • Communicate with you about your account, updates, or support requests
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security threats
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data in these limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Third-party services that help us operate the platform (e.g., MongoDB Atlas for data storage, InkSoft for merchandise fulfillment)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Wordeth and its users
  • Aggregated Data: We may share anonymized, aggregated analytics that cannot identify individual users

4. Data Retention

  • Account Data: Retained as long as your account is active. Deleted upon account deletion or data flush request.
  • Usage Events: Automatically deleted after 90 days from our primary database. Aggregated (anonymized) summaries may be archived for trend analysis.
  • Archived Analytics: Daily summaries stored in cloud storage contain aggregate counts only — no personally identifiable information.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

5.1 What We Use

  • Essential Storage: Authentication tokens (localStorage) to keep you signed in
  • Preference Storage: Cookie consent choice, UI preferences
  • Analytics Tracking: Usage events sent to our servers to understand how features are used (only with your consent)

5.2 Your Choices

When you first visit Wordeth, a consent banner lets you choose:

  • Accept All: Enables analytics tracking alongside essential functionality
  • Essential Only: Limits data collection to what is strictly necessary for the Service to function

You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser storage and revisiting the site. The consent banner will reappear.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Deletion ("Right to Be Forgotten"): Request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Data Portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Withdraw Consent: Opt out of non-essential data collection at any time
  • Object: Object to processing of your data for certain purposes

How to Exercise Your Rights

  • Self-Service: Delete your account through your profile settings
  • Contact Us: Email privacy@wordeth.com with your request. We will respond within 30 days.
  • Data Flush: If you contact us requesting data deletion, we will permanently remove your account, usage history, content, social connections, and uploaded files from our systems.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encrypted passwords (bcrypt hashing)
  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • JWT-based authentication with expiring tokens
  • Rate limiting to prevent abuse
  • Content Security Policy headers
  • Regular security reviews

No system is perfectly secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Wordeth is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at privacy@wordeth.com and we will promptly delete it.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed and stored in countries outside your own. We use MongoDB Atlas and AWS cloud services which may store data in various regions. By using the Service, you consent to the transfer and processing of your data in these locations.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify registered users of material changes. The "Effective Date" at the top indicates the latest revision. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, requests, or concerns:

Email: privacy@wordeth.com

Data Protection Requests: privacy@wordeth.com (subject line: "Privacy Rights Request")
