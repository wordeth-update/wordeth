Documentation

How Label Setup Works

The label setup system lets you onboard your artist roster and upload template artwork for merchandise production. This is the first step to getting your artists' merch live on Wordeth.

  • Artist Roster: Upload a CSV with your artist names and genres. The system automatically generates unique IDs and identifiers for each artist. Existing artists (same name) are skipped to prevent duplicates.
  • Template Artwork: Upload artwork files per artist for use on merchandise. Files should be high-resolution (300 DPI) with transparent backgrounds, or vector format.
  • Sales Tracking: Once your artists are set up and merch is live, all sales data is tracked automatically through our in-house fulfillment system. No manual data entry required.

Key Concepts

Artist ID
A unique identifier automatically generated for each artist when they are added to your roster. You'll see this in your dashboard and it's used internally to track everything.
Template Artwork
The design files uploaded per artist that serve as the base artwork for merchandise. These should be print-ready at 300 DPI or in vector format (SVG, PDF, EPS, AI).
Revenue Share
Your label's percentage of merchandise sales, configured by your account administrator. Displayed as a decimal (e.g., 0.15 = 15%).

Common Mistakes

  • Low-resolution artwork: Images below 300 DPI will not print cleanly on merchandise. Always use print-quality files.
  • JPEG artwork: JPEG compression causes artifacts in printing. Use PNG with transparency or vector formats instead.
  • Missing artist_name column: The CSV header row must contain "artist_name" exactly as written.
  • Special characters in CSVs: If an artist name contains commas, wrap it in double quotes: "Anderson .Paak, Yes Lawd"

Recommended Workflow

  1. Step 1: Log in to the Partner Portal with your credentials.
  2. Step 2: Upload your artist roster CSV (just names and genres).
  3. Step 3: Upload template artwork for each artist from the Artwork tab.
  4. Step 4: Visit the dashboard to see your label overview and artist breakdown.
  5. Step 5: Share specific views with team members using the Share Dashboard feature.

Artwork File Guidelines

  • Best format: PNG with transparent background at 300 DPI, or SVG/PDF vector file.
  • Dimensions: Design artwork at actual print size (typically 10-12 inches wide for chest prints).
  • Color: CMYK color mode is preferred for accurate print colors. sRGB is accepted and will be converted.
  • Fonts: All text must be outlined/converted to paths in vector files.
  • Max file size: 50 MB per file. If you have very large Photoshop files, flatten layers before uploading.
