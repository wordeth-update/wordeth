Support

How to reach us.

Questions, problems, abuse reports, account requests: one address, read every day. support@wordeth.com

Report someone or something

In the app, open the person, message, room or photo and choose Report. On the website, use the Report link on a profile or in a room. Every report is reviewed and acted on within 24 hours. If you would rather write to us, email the address above with what you saw and where.

Block someone

Open their profile and choose Block. They can no longer message you, join rooms you host, or see your profile, and you will not see theirs. Unblock from the same place.

Delete your account

In the app: Profile → Delete account. On the website: Profile → Delete account. Your profile, messages, photos, replays and rooms history are removed. Purchases already made are not refunded by deletion. If you cannot sign in, email us from the address on the account and we will delete it for you.

Payments and tokens

Token packs bought through the App Store or Google Play are refunded by Apple or Google under their rules. Anything bought on wordeth.com, and merch orders, are handled by us: write with the email on the account and the date.

Something is broken

Tell us what you were doing, what happened, and which phone. A screenshot helps. We answer within one business day.
