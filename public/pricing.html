Plans & pricing

The rooms, the lyrics and the merch are yours the minute you join, and no card is asked for. A plan is for the day you want more: a room that charges at the door, a place to sell your designs, a home for your music.

    What you are not signing up for

    Free is not a trial

    The free plan has no clock on it and asks for no card. Stay on it for a week or for good.

    No trapdoor on the way out

    Cancel from your subscription page whenever you like. You keep what you paid for until the period ends, and you are not charged again.

    No surprise at the door

    A paid room shows its price in tokens before you go in. Nothing leaves your balance until you say yes.

    Your card stays with Stripe

    Checkout runs on Stripe's own page. Wordeth never sees or stores your card number.

    Fair questions

    Do I have to pay to use Wordeth?

    No. Every free room, the lyric search and the merch shop are open to you on the free plan, and you can open free rooms of your own.

    So what does User+ change?

    Two things. You can go into rooms that charge at the door, and you can open one yourself and set its price. The plan also adds 50 tokens to your balance when you join and each time it renews.

    What is a token?

    Tokens are what a paid room charges at the door. The host sets the price and the tokens you pay go to them. Packs are 25 for $1.99, 50 for $3.49 and 100 for $5.99.

    What is a Wildcard?

    A look inside before you decide. After 8 active hours on the free plan you get one Wildcard: three free minutes in one paid room of your choosing.

    What if I stop using it?

    Then cancel. It takes a moment, there is no exit interview, and the plan stays on until the end of the period you already paid for.

    Can I switch plans?

    Yes. Choose the new one and it starts straight away. The old one ends at the same moment, so you are never billed for two.

    Monthly or yearly?

    User+ is $3.99 a month or $39 a year. Yearly costs less in dollars. The 50 tokens come with each renewal, so monthly adds them twelve times a year and yearly adds them once. Other plans are billed monthly.

    How long can a designer stay free?

    Until it is working. Explore is free until you reach $100 in earnings, 10 sales or 3 active months, whichever comes first. After that you pick a paid plan to keep selling.

    The door is open either way.
