Fair questions

What does User+ change?

You can go into paid rooms, and open one of your own with a price at the door. The tokens people pay go to you. 50 tokens come with each renewal.

What is a token?

What a paid room charges at the door. Packs: 25 for $1.99, 50 for $3.49, 100 for $5.99.

What is a Wildcard?

After 8 active hours on the free plan: three free minutes inside one paid room, so you can look before you pay.

Switching or stopping?

A new plan starts at once and the old one ends, so you never pay for two. Cancel whenever you like; no exit interview.
