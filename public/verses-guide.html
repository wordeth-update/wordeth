Getting Started

Create a Room

Tap the Create Room button on the Verses page. Give your room a name and optional description. You automatically become the host.

Join a Room

Browse active rooms and tap to join. You enter the crowd first and can listen in. Raise your hand to request to speak!

Room Roles

Host controls the room. Speakers are on stage with their mic. Crowd members listen and can chat or raise their hand.

The Action Bar

The bottom bar is your control center. Here's what each button does:

Mic

Toggle your microphone on/off. When muted, a red slash appears. You must be a speaker or host to use this.

Effects

Apply voice filters to your microphone! Choose from effects like echo, deep voice, robot, and more to make your audio unique.

Karaoke

Open the karaoke panel to search for songs and sing along with synced lyrics. The host must enable karaoke mode first.

Camera

Turn on your camera to appear in the video grid. The host controls whether camera is available (Off / Ask / Open).

Photo

Share images in the room chat. Pick a photo from your device and it appears in the conversation for everyone to see.

Hand

Raise your hand to request to speak. The host will see your request and can promote you from the crowd to the stage.

Chat

Open the room chat to send text messages. Great for sharing thoughts, links, or reacting without interrupting the speaker.

Host Controls

If you're the host, you have special powers to manage the room experience:

Video Mode

Off Ask Open

Off: No one can use camera. Ask: Participants request permission first. Open: Anyone can turn on camera freely.

Karaoke

Toggle karaoke mode on or off. When enabled, participants can search for songs and sing along with synchronized lyrics displayed on screen.

Lock / Unlock

Lock the room to prevent new people from joining. Useful when your room is full or you want a private conversation.

Mute All

Instantly mute every participant. Helpful for getting everyone's attention or resetting the conversation.

Close Room

End the room session for everyone. All participants will be disconnected and the room will be removed.

Managing Participants

Tap the three-dot menu on any participant's avatar to move them between stage and crowd, or remove them from the room entirely.

Video & Camera

Video Grid

Up to 6 people can be on camera at once. The grid adapts automatically: 1 person fills the screen, 2 go side-by-side, 3-4 form a 2x2 grid, and 5-6 fill a 2x3 grid.

AR Face Filters

When your camera is on, you can apply fun genre-themed face filters that overlay effects on your face in real time. Express yourself with style!

Speaker Priority

When more than 6 people want to use video, unmuted speakers get priority. Active speakers are always visible in the grid.

How Video Modes Work

Video: Off

Camera button greyed out for everyone

Video: Ask

Tap Camera to send a request to the host

Video: Open

Anyone can turn on camera freely

Karaoke Mode

1

Host Enables Karaoke

The host taps "Karaoke: Off" in host controls to turn it on. The karaoke button in everyone's action bar becomes active.

2

Search for a Song

Tap the Karaoke button to open the search panel. Type the song name and pick from the results.

3

Sing Along

Lyrics scroll on screen in sync with the music. Your microphone audio and the song mix together so everyone in the room hears both!

4

Voice Effects

Add voice effects while singing for even more fun. Try echo, reverb, or pitch shift to get creative with your performance!

Chat & Photo Sharing

Live Chat

The chat panel runs alongside the audio room. Send messages, react to what's happening, and keep the conversation going without needing to unmute. System messages also appear here when things happen in the room.

Photo Sharing

Tap the Photo button to share an image from your device. Photos appear directly in the chat for everyone to see. Great for sharing album art, concert photos, memes, or anything visual!

Tips & Tricks

Stay Connected

If you navigate to another page while in a room, a mini-player appears at the bottom so you stay connected and can return anytime.

Invite Friends

Use the share button on your room to invite friends. They'll get a notification and can join with one tap.

Use Headphones

For the best experience (especially during karaoke), use headphones to avoid echo and feedback.

Trending Topics

Check the "Trending This Week" bar on the Verses page for conversation starters. Hot, new, and viral topics to spark great discussions!

Host Transfer

Hosts can transfer hosting duties to another participant if they need to step away. The room keeps going!

Mix Audio

You can play local audio files and have them stream into the room. Perfect for DJs or sharing tracks with everyone live.
