CONTEXT / PRODUCT INTENT
Wordeth Verses is a HYBRID EXPRESSION ENGINE centered on independent artists performing THEIR OWN original lyrics (not covers). Fans participate by voting and interpreting, and designers can create merch designs tied to winning verses (merch fulfillment handled externally by Commerce OpCo). Verses Tournaments/Championships create long-term demand through ritual cycles: theme announcement, submission window, live showcases, voting windows, winners, and merch drops.

PRIMARY GOALS
1) Implement a recurring tournament system (“Seasons” and “Rounds”) with configurable cadence.
2) Support three participation roles:
   - Independent Artists (submit/perform original verses)
   - Fans/Users (watch, vote, react, follow)
   - Designers (optional in MVP: submit designs tied to verses; at minimum provide hooks/events for future)
3) Provide sponsorship inventory and placement (naming rights, sound logos, leaderboard sponsorships, round sponsorships).
4) Build dashboards and admin tooling to create/manage tournaments, approve content, moderate, and report.
5) Integrate with existing WebRTC live rooms for showcases, and integrate with “wordeth-ads-standalone” for ad delivery on lyric pages AND sponsorship placements within Verses.

NON-GOALS (for MVP)
- Do NOT implement full payout disbursement; only track “payout owed” placeholders and ledger events.
- Do NOT implement fully automated music rights licensing; assume independent artists own their lyrics and accept ToS.
- Do NOT build a complex recommendation engine; simple sorting and featured picks are fine.

TOURNAMENT CONCEPT (WHAT IT IS)
- A “Season” consists of multiple “Rounds” (e.g., Round of 32, Round of 16, Quarterfinals, Finals).
- Each Round has:
  - A theme (e.g., “Redemption”, “Betrayal”, “Victory”)
  - Submission window (artists submit original verse + lyrics)
  - Showcase event (WebRTC stage rooms)
  - Voting window (fans vote + reactions)
  - Winner selection rules (configurable)
- Output: winners get featured placement, “Hall of Verse” entries, and optional merch drops hooks.

HOW IT WORKS (RITUAL CADENCE)
Implement a default weekly cadence (configurable):
- Monday: Theme announced
- Wednesday: Submissions close
- Thursday: Showcase events (live WebRTC rooms)
- Saturday: Voting closes
- Sunday: Winners announced + merch hook event fired
Support also monthly “Championships” made of weekly rounds.

VOTING SYSTEM (IMPORTANT)
- Voting should be designed to reduce pure popularity gaming:
  - Rate-limited voting: 1 vote per account per matchup per round (or weighted by reputation later)
  - Anti-abuse: device fingerprint optional, IP throttling, suspicious voting flagging
  - Optional multi-factor scoring (configurable):
    - Performance/Delivery (0–5)
    - Originality (0–5)
    - Theme Fit (0–5)
    - Crowd Reaction (likes/cheers)
  - MVP can start with simple “vote for A or B” + “reaction” counts, but structure the schema to support weighted scoring later.
