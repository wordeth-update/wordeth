How It Works

Wordeth ads use contextual keyword matching to connect your brand with relevant music content. When a user searches for lyrics, our system checks for ads with matching keywords and displays them alongside the results.

1

Create Your Account

Register for an advertiser account through our registration page. Choose between Self-Serve or Partner (White Glove) account types.

2

Design Your Ad

Create your ad creative following our size specifications. Upload your image and set your click-through URL.

3

Choose Your Keywords

Select up to 25 keywords that relate to your brand. Your ad will appear when users search for lyrics containing these terms.

4

Submit for Review

Our team reviews all ads within 24-48 hours. Once approved, your ad goes live automatically.

Image Requirements

  • Maximum file size: 150 KB
  • Accepted formats: JPG, PNG, or GIF (animated GIFs allowed)
  • Image must be hosted on your own domain or a reliable image hosting service

Keyword Targeting

Keyword targeting is how your ads reach the right audience. Your ad is shown when a user searches for lyrics that match one or more of your keywords.

How Keyword Matching Works

When a user searches for a song or lyrics, our system checks their search query against your keyword list. If there's a match, your ad is eligible to appear alongside the results.

Keyword Examples

Ice Cream Brand

ice cream, summer, sweet, cold, treat, dessert, frozen, vanilla, chocolate, strawberry

These keywords would match searches for songs about summer, sweets, or treats.

Headphone Company

music, beats, sound, bass, listen, headphones, audio, volume, rhythm, vibe

Music-related terms naturally appear in many song searches.

Streetwear Brand

style, fresh, drip, fashion, flex, swag, outfit, wear, cool, trend

Slang and culture-related terms that appear in hip-hop and pop lyrics.

Keyword Tips

  • Use up to 25 keywords per ad
  • Think about words that naturally appear in song lyrics
  • Include product names, related themes, and common associations
  • Mix broad terms (e.g., "music") with specific ones (e.g., "headphones")
  • Consider genre-specific slang and terminology
  • Separate keywords with commas

Best Practices

Follow these guidelines to create effective ads that engage Wordeth's music-loving audience.

Use High-Quality Images

Clear, professional images grab attention. Avoid pixelated or stretched graphics. Make sure your brand is easily recognizable.

Include a Clear Call to Action

Tell users what you want them to do: "Shop Now," "Learn More," "Get 20% Off." A strong CTA drives higher click-through rates.

Match Your Landing Page

Make sure your click-through URL leads to a page relevant to your ad. Don't send users to a generic homepage if your ad promotes a specific product.

Choose Relevant Keywords

Pick keywords that genuinely relate to your brand. Irrelevant keywords waste impressions and annoy users.

Keep Text Minimal

Banner ads have limited space. Use short, punchy copy that communicates your message at a glance.

Consider Your Audience

Wordeth users are music enthusiasts. Ads that connect with music culture tend to perform better.

Review Process

Every ad submitted to Wordeth goes through a review process to ensure quality and policy compliance.

Submitted

Your ad enters the review queue. You can track its status in your Ad Portal dashboard.

Under Review

Our team checks your ad creative, landing page, and keywords against our content policy. This typically takes 24-48 hours.

Approved & Live

Once approved, your ad starts appearing in relevant lyric searches immediately. You can monitor performance in your dashboard.

Common Rejection Reasons

  • Image doesn't match the required dimensions
  • Broken or unreachable click-through URL
  • Ad contains prohibited content
  • Misleading or deceptive claims
  • Low-quality or blurry image
  • Landing page doesn't match the ad content

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to advertise on Wordeth?

Wordeth advertising is currently offered on a flat-rate basis. Contact our team for current pricing information or visit the registration page to apply.

How long does account approval take?

New advertiser accounts are typically reviewed and approved within 48-72 hours of submission.

Can I edit my ad after submitting it?

Currently, you would need to create a new ad with the updated content. Future updates will include in-place editing.

How many ads can I run at once?

There is no limit on the number of ads you can have active. Each ad can target different keywords to reach different audiences.

What if my ad is rejected?

If your ad is rejected, you'll be able to see the reason in your dashboard. You can create a new ad addressing the issues and resubmit.

Can I see how my ads are performing?

Yes. Your Ad Portal dashboard shows impressions, clicks, and other engagement metrics for each of your ads.

Do ads appear on mobile?

Yes. If you upload a mobile-sized banner (320x50), it will appear on mobile devices. Desktop leaderboard ads (728x90) appear on larger screens.
