Keyword Targeting

Keywords are the foundation of our contextual advertising system. When a user searches for lyrics, we check if any of your keywords appear in their search query.

How to Choose Keywords

  • Be specific: "chocolate ice cream" is better than just "food"
  • Think like your customer: What would they search for?
  • Include variations: "car, cars, driving, road, highway"
  • Consider song themes: What songs mention your product category?

Keyword Examples

Ice Cream Brand

ice cream, frozen, summer, sweet, cold, dessert, treat, vanilla, chocolate, cone, scoop, sundae, melt

This ad would show when someone searches for "Ice Cream Man" by Master P or "Ice Cream" by Selena Gomez.

Car Dealership

car, cars, driving, drive, road, highway, wheels, ride, motor, speed, fast, cruise, convertible

This ad would show for searches containing driving themes common in hip-hop and rock songs.

Fashion Brand

fashion, style, clothes, outfit, drip, fresh, fly, designer, shoes, sneakers, dress, wear

Fashion keywords appear frequently in pop, hip-hop, and R&B lyrics.

Best Practices

Keep it Simple

Your ad has limited space. Use a clear image, your logo, and a simple message.

Strong Call-to-Action

Tell users what to do: "Shop Now", "Learn More", "Get Started"

Mobile-First Design

Many users browse on mobile. Ensure your ad is readable on smaller screens.

Relevant Landing Pages

Link to a page that matches your ad's message for better conversion.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does advertising cost?

Wordeth uses a flexible pricing model. Contact our sales team for current rates or sign up for self-serve access.

Can I update my ad after it's approved?

Yes, you can edit your ad's title, description, and keywords. Changes to the image or URL may require re-approval.

How many keywords should I use?

We recommend using 10-20 relevant keywords. Using too few may limit your reach, while using unrelated keywords may waste impressions.

Why was my ad rejected?

Common reasons include policy violations, low image quality, broken links, or misleading content. You can edit and resubmit your ad.

Can I pause my ad?

Yes, you can pause and resume your ad at any time from your dashboard.
