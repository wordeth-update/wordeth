Documentation

How Bulk Upload Works

The bulk upload system lets you import your label catalog and sales data using standard CSV files. This is the fastest way to get your data into Wordeth's partner dashboard.

  • Artists: Upload a CSV of your artists to add them to your label's roster. If you upload again, existing artists (same slug) are skipped and only new ones are added.
  • Sales Data: Upload sales records in bulk. Each row becomes a sale entry linked to your label. Geographic data powers the interactive sales heatmap.

Key Concepts

Slug
A URL-safe version of a name. Use lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens only. For example: "Dreamville Records" becomes "dreamville", and "J. Cole" becomes "j-cole".
Revenue Share
A decimal between 0 and 1 representing your label's percentage. Use 0.15 for 15%, 0.20 for 20%, etc.
SKU
Stock Keeping Unit — a unique product identifier. Example: "FHD-HOOD-BLK-L" for a Forest Hills Drive Hoodie in Black, Large.
Lyrics Snippet
The exact lyric text printed on merchandise. This powers the Lyrics Leaderboard and "Coined" phrase detection (phrases with 400+ total makes are flagged as Coined).

Common Mistakes

  • Inconsistent slugs: Make sure the same artist always has the same slug. "j-cole" and "jcole" would be treated as two different artists.
  • Missing required columns: The header row must contain all required column names exactly as specified.
  • Incorrect date format: Use YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2025-01-15). Other formats may not parse correctly.
  • Revenue share as percentage: Enter 0.15, not 15. The system expects a decimal between 0 and 1.
  • Special characters in CSVs: If a field contains commas, wrap it in double quotes: "First things first, rest in peace"

Recommended Workflow

  1. Step 1: Log in to the Partner Portal with your credentials.
  2. Step 2: Upload your Artists CSV to add your roster to the label.
  3. Step 3: Upload your Sales Data CSV to populate the dashboard with real numbers.
  4. Step 4: Use the dashboard to explore revenue trends, artist breakdowns, and the geographic sales heatmap.
  5. Step 5: Share specific views with team members using the Share Dashboard feature.

Geographic Data Tips

To get the most out of the sales heatmap:

  • Include country, city, and country_code at minimum.
  • Add lat and lng coordinates for precise map markers. Without them, the city won't appear on the heatmap.
  • Use the AI prompt helper tab to have ChatGPT generate coordinates for your cities automatically.

Sales Data CSV Prompt

order_id,artist_name,artist_slug,product_name,sku,quantity,unit_price,product_type,song_title,album_title,lyrics_snippet,sale_date,country,country_code,region,city,lat,lng

Rules:
- order_id: Unique identifier per order (e.g., "ORD-2025-001")
- artist_slug: lowercase, hyphens only (e.g., "j-cole", "kendrick-lamar")
- sku: Unique product code using format like TYPE-PRODUCT-COLOR-SIZE (e.g., "HOOD-FHD-BLK-L")
- quantity: Positive integer
- unit_price: Numeric price per unit, no currency symbols (e.g., 65.00)
- product_type: hoodie, t-shirt, hat, poster, vinyl, accessories, or other apparel types
- song_title and album_title: The song/album associated with the merch design (leave blank if not applicable)
- lyrics_snippet: The exact lyric text printed on the merchandise (leave blank if none). If the text contains commas, wrap the entire field in double quotes.
- sale_date: Format as YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2025-01-15)
- country_code: ISO 2-letter code (US, GB, CA, JP, DE, etc.)
- lat/lng: Geographic coordinates for the city. Look up approximate coordinates for each city. For example: New York → 40.71, -74.01 / London → 51.51, -0.13 / Tokyo → 35.68, 139.69

Here is my sales data to format:

[PASTE YOUR RAW DATA HERE - e.g., a spreadsheet export, order list, or text description of your recent sales with product names, quantities, prices, locations, etc.]
